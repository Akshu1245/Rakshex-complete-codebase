# RakshEx Refund and Cancellation Policy

Effective date: 12 July 2026

This page states the current private-beta access position. It does not create a self-serve purchase contract.

## Private beta

RaksHex cannot take money this week. Access is by waitlist, evaluation, or invite. Evaluation prices may be shown for planning. They are not an offer to sell and do not start a paid subscription.

There is no live checkout, tax or GST collection, auto-renewal, or self-serve purchase.

## Paid access

Paid access, if and when offered, is by invite or an executed Order Form only. An Order Form controls fees, taxes, term, cancellation, and any credits for that purchase.

Self-serve cancellation, renewal, payment-failure, and refund terms do not apply this week because self-serve purchases are not offered.

## Consumer rights and contact

Nothing here limits a mandatory right under applicable consumer law. Contact legal@rakshex.in with the workspace, the reason, and the requested remedy. Do not email card, bank, provider-key, or password values.

# Legal Review Source Notes — 2026-07-30

This is a source index for qualified review, not legal advice or a statement
that every provision applies to Rakshex. Applicability, commencement dates,
contract wording, notices, retention, and incident duties must be confirmed by
counsel for the actual entity, customers, processing, and jurisdictions.

## Official India sources checked

## Review questions

- Which DPDP Act and Rules provisions are in force on the intended launch date,
  and what phased commencement dates affect notices, consent, rights, breach
  response, children, processors, and significant data fiduciaries?
- Is Rakshex acting as data fiduciary, processor, or both for each product flow?
- Which CERT-In reporting, log-retention, time-synchronization, and cooperation
  duties apply to the entity and its service model?
- Which consumer, intermediary, tax/GST, employment, payment, cross-border,
  sectoral, and contractual regimes also apply?
- Do the Privacy Policy, DPA, MSA, incident plan, retention settings,
  subprocessors, and actual product behavior agree?
